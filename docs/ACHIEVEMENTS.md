## How Boop Type Unlocks Work

When you start the game, you can only do basic boops. As you unlock achievements, you gain access to new boop TYPES to try. For example:

- You start with: Basic finger boop, basic location boops
- After "First Boop": Still basic
- After 5 boops: Unlock hand/finger variations (Knuckle Boop, Pinky Boop, etc.)
- After 10 boops: Unlock location variations (Bellyboop, Ear Boop, etc.)
- After specific achievements (like Sneak Attack): Unlock pose/style variations (Ninja Boop, etc.)
- After mastering basic types: Unlock combo variations (The Combo, Synchronized Boop, etc.)

Each time you do a new boop type, the app recognizes it and gives you an achievement for that boop type. This means:
- The game gets deeper as you play
- There's always something new to try and unlock
- Booping becomes more creative and fun over time
- Higher-level boops need specific conditions (timing, location, multiple people)

## The Core Loop (how you record a boop)

Three taps, and you're done. Speed matters because you are usually fleeing.

1. Home screen is just the big BOOP button. No feed, nothing to scroll.
2. Tap it: pick who you booped. Recent people at the top, plus "someone else" for people not in the app.
3. Pick which kind of boop it was. Unlocked types are tappable, locked ones show greyed out (seeing them is part of the motivation).
4. Optional photo attaches here, pulled from the camera roll.
5. Finish: phone buzzes, and if you unlocked an achievement, confetti falls and the badge appears.

Picking the boop type stays a deliberate step. It's part of the fun, not friction to optimize away.

## Photos and Witnesses

The photo is taken by a THIRD PERSON mid-boop, not a selfie after the fact. This means:
- Photos come from the camera roll, not from inside the app
- Whoever took the photo is a witness, and can be tagged
- Witnesses get credit, which opens a whole achievement category for being the camera person
- A witness photo is stronger proof than anything self-reported

## Powerups: Free Boops and Shields

Two powerups, one for offense and one for defense.

FREE BOOP (offense): Used to overrule someone who refuses to confirm a boop you actually landed. You can only spend it on a boop you already recorded and they already denied. This is deliberately NOT a "skip verification" button, because that version could be abused from the couch to record boops that never happened.

BOOP SHIELD (defense): Blocks one incoming boop. The next boop someone lands on you doesn't count against your record. Shields are what make the Elusive achievement into a real strategy instead of luck.

RULES:
- You can hold 3 free boops and 3 shields at once (6 total, tracked separately)
- Hard cap at 3 each. No stockpiling.
- Refill to full on the 1st of every month, for everyone at the same time
- They don't expire, but since the cap blocks hoarding, the smart play is to spend them before the month rolls over
- Some achievements award free boops or shields (so achievements GIVE you something, not just a badge)
